Attendance Tracking

Payroll Processing

Expense Management

Employee Self-Service Portal

Announcements

Push company-wide announcements to all employees instantly. Supports urgent, info, and general notice types.

Direct Messaging

Live Dashboard

Today's attendance at a glance, pending expense approvals, unread messages — your whole team status in one screen.

Secure & Private

Document Storage

Store employee contracts, ID documents, training certificates, and HR files securely. Employees can view their own documents from the portal.

Smart Reminders

Automated alerts for payroll deadlines, pending expense approvals, attendance gaps, and contract renewals — so nothing falls through the cracks.
